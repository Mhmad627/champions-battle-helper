About

Champions Battle Helper answers one question fast: what beats the Pokémon in front of me? Type its name and you get the move types that hit it hardest, the ones to avoid, and warnings about abilities that change the result.

It's built for Pokémon Champions and for players who don't want to memorise the type chart. Pokémon that aren't in Champions yet are still listed, with a note.

Where the data comes from

  • Pokémon, types and abilities come from PokéAPI, and the Pokémon icons from PokéAPI's sprite collection.
  • The Champions roster is based on Serebii's list of available Pokémon and may lag behind game updates.
  • Type matchups follow the current rules (Generation VI and later).

Game updates can change rosters and abilities before this site catches up. If something here disagrees with the game, trust the game.

Privacy

This site doesn't use accounts, cookies, analytics or ads, and it doesn't send anything you type anywhere. Searches happen entirely on your device.

A few small things are saved in your browser's local storage so the site remembers them next time:

  • your light or dark theme choice
  • your last six Pokémon lookups
  • the team you're checking, and which view you picked for it

If you install the site or visit it once, your browser also keeps a copy of its files so it works offline. Clearing your browser's site data removes all of this.
