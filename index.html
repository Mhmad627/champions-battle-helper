        Weather & terrain

        Some moves and abilities change the battlefield for a few turns. Cards that help the Pokémon you looked up are marked.

        WeatherLasts 5 turns, or 8 if the Pokémon holds the matching rock.

        Extreme weatherOnly from Primal Groudon, Primal Kyogre and Mega Rayquaza. It can't be replaced by normal weather.

        TerrainLasts 5 turns, or 8 with a Terrain Extender.

        Terrain only affects Pokémon. Only one terrain can be up at a time, and a new one replaces the old. The type boost was 1.5× in Gen VII and has been 1.3× since Gen VIII.
